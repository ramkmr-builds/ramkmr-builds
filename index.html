🧭Getting started4 quick steps
  1. Register as a Citizen, or apply as a Volunteer.
  2. Report a pollution source with a photo and location.
  3. Volunteers accept the cleanup and add an after photo.
  4. Everyone earns points and badges.
👤About guest modePrivate, on your own device
Guest mode is a private space on your device. You cannot see real members or other guests, and nothing you do changes the real website.
